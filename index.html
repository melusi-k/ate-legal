What's inside

Keep the recipes your camera roll keeps losing.

Everything after "that looks good" is handled for you: from saving and scaling to shopping and the final timer.

  • Shopping list

    One list, sorted by aisle

    Add several recipes and ate merges the duplicates and groups them by aisle, so you shop once and in order.

  • Library

    A library you can actually search

    Filters, collections, and a roomy landscape grid for browsing on the counter.

  • Scale

    ×1, ×2, ×3 in one tap

    Cooking for two tonight and six on Sunday? Quantities update for you.

  • Cook mode

    Big text, hands-free

    One step at a time, with built-in timers. Say "next", "back" or "start timer" when your hands are covered in flour.

  • Timers

    Timers that follow you

    Live Activities on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, plus a sticky timer bar while you browse the app.

  • Share

    One clean link

    Send a recipe without the ads and pop-ups. Friends open it and save it in a tap.

  • Sync

    On every device you use

    Sign in and your library, lists and edits follow you across devices.

  • Private

    Private by design

    The AI runs on your iPhone. No ads, no tracking, no selling your dinner plans.

  • Coming soon

    Apple Watch companion

    Steps and timers on your wrist, so your phone can stay out of the sauce.

How ate works

Three steps from "ooh" to "ate".

  1. Drop it in

    Paste a link or caption, upload a screenshot or photo, or type what you're craving.

  2. ate writes the card

    Apple Intelligence on your iPhone lays out the ingredients and steps. Tweak anything before you save.

  3. Cook from it

    Scale it, shop for it, then follow along in cook mode with timers and voice control.
