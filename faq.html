Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Does ate sell my data?

No. ate does not sell, rent, or share your personal data for advertising or marketing purposes.

Does ate use tracking?

ate does not use ad networks or analytics SDKs for user tracking. The app is designed to be private, and recipe AI runs on-device using Apple Intelligence.

What happens when I delete my account?

Your account and synced data are deleted when you delete your account inside the app, subject to normal processing time and backup retention limits. Local data on a device remains until you delete it or delete the app.

What about shared recipe links?

Shared recipe links are read-only copies and may remain available until the sender removes them or the link expires. If you need a link removed, contact the developer with the link.

Does ate use AI?

Yes, recipe parsing is processed on-device using Apple Intelligence for local recipe extraction and card generation. ate does not send your recipe content to a third-party AI service as part of the normal recipe generation flow.

Can I export my recipes?

Export tools are provided in the app when available. In the Privacy Policy, the app also sets out data access and portability rights for personal data, where applicable.

How do I make a privacy request?

Use the dedicated request page or email the developer directly with the type of request you want to make.

Is ate suitable for children?

ate is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

What if I find a bug or security issue?

Use the contact details below to report security concerns or product bugs. Please do not share private account data in public issues or social channels if it is sensitive.
