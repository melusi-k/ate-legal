Types of requests

  • Access a copy of your personal information.
  • Correct inaccurate personal information.
  • Delete your account and associated synced data.
  • Export your data in a portable format, where available.
  • Object to or restrict processing, where applicable.
  • Request information about the data held about you.

What to include

For a faster response, include the email address used for sign-in, any relevant account details, and the specific rights request you want to make.

Response timelines

ate aims to reply within 30 days to privacy and data rights requests. Where required by law, requests may be handled under the applicable data protection framework in the region in which the user resides.
