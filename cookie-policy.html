Cookie Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

ate is designed to be private by default. This page explains how cookies and similar technologies are used on the ate website and in the app experience.

What we do not do

  • ate does not use advertising trackers.
  • ate does not sell personal data.
  • ate does not use third-party analytics cookies for marketing or behavioural profiling.
  • Recipe AI runs on-device using Apple Intelligence and is not routed through a public ad-tech or analytics stack.

Cookies and local storage

For the static GitHub Pages website, cookies are not used for advertising or analytics. Any browser storage that does exist is limited to functional elements necessary for the site to work smoothly, such as basic session state, if used in the future.

For the app itself, ate may store local data on-device for its own functionality, such as recipes, settings, shopping lists, cache, and sync state. That data stays on the user device unless the user signs in to sync their account.

Necessary technology

Service functionality may rely on standard browser or platform-level storage to provide features such as local caching, temporary state, and reliability. These are not used to track users across websites or apps.

Shared recipe links

Shared recipe links are served with standard web hosting and may use normal web server logs for operational troubleshooting, service reliability, and abuse detection. These logs are not used for advertising or profiling, and are not used to build an ad profile of individuals.

Consent and your controls

If you use the app or website and want to manage preferences or remove local data, you can do so from the app settings or by deleting local app data on your device. If you have specific questions about a data request or a privacy concern, contact the developer.

Changes

Any future change to this Cookie Policy will be reflected in the date above and described in the app where needed.
